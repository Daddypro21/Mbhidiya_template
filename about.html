About Mbhidiya Intelligence

Intelligence Beyond What Is Obvious

An intelligence and investigations firm helping organisations make better decisions in complex, high-risk and cross-border environments.

Our Mission

Reduce Uncertainty. Support Decisions.

We work where conventional information becomes insufficient.

Our role is not simply to collect data. Our role is to help clients understand what the information means, what may be missing, what does not add up, who is connected to whom, where hidden risk may exist, and what requires further verification.

We combine open-source intelligence, investigative methodology, corporate research, digital intelligence, network analysis and strategic assessment.

The result is intelligence designed to support decisions.

Our role is to help clients understand:
  • What the information means
  • What may be missing
  • What does not add up
  • Who is connected to whom
  • Where hidden risk may exist
  • What requires further verification

To help organisations reduce uncertainty by providing clear, defensible and actionable intelligence about people, companies, relationships and markets.

Our Approach

Five Steps to Clarity

1

Define the Decision

Every investigation starts with a practical question. What decision does the client need to make?

2

Identify the Intelligence Gap

We determine what the client knows, what remains unclear and what information could materially affect the decision.

3

Investigate

We use appropriate research and investigative methods to collect, verify and analyse relevant information.

4

Connect the Dots

Information rarely exists in one place. We examine people, companies, ownership structures, digital footprints, relationships and other relevant connections.

5

Deliver an Assessment

We do not simply provide a collection of search results. We provide an assessment of the relevant intelligence, identified risks, information gaps and recommended next steps.
