Continuous Intelligence Monitoring

Risk Does Not End When the Deal Is Signed.

A person or company that presents no significant concerns today may develop new exposure tomorrow.

Mbhidiya Intelligence can provide ongoing monitoring of selected individuals, companies or counterparties for relevant changes and emerging information.

Monitoring May Include

Adverse media Corporate changes Leadership changes New affiliations Litigation developments Regulatory developments Reputation developments Relevant digital activity Other agreed intelligence indicators

This service is designed for organisations managing ongoing relationships with high-value or high-risk counterparties.
