Intelligence for High-Stakes Decisions

Business decisions often depend on trust. You hire an executive. You acquire a company. You select a local partner. You enter a new market. You appoint an intermediary. You invest capital. You rely on a supplier.

The information available on paper may look reassuring. But public profiles, corporate registrations, references and standard due diligence do not always reveal the full picture.

Who are we really dealing with?
Who controls the company?
Who influences the decision-makers?
What information is missing?
What risks are hidden beneath the surface?
What could become our problem after we sign the deal?

Mbhidiya Intelligence helps organisations identify the people, relationships, interests, reputational issues and intelligence gaps that may affect a decision.

We turn fragmented information into a clearer understanding of risk.

Canada & Africa

Mbhidiya Intelligence supports organisations operating in Canada, Africa and cross-border commercial environments connecting the two.

Our work is particularly relevant to organisations dealing with unfamiliar counterparties, new markets, complex ownership structures and high-trust relationships.

The African Continental Free Trade Area is intended to deepen intra-African trade, investment and regional value chains. As organisations expand across borders, they increasingly need to make decisions about people and companies they have never worked with before.

The easier it becomes to connect markets, the more important it becomes to understand who operates within them.
